Welcome to the official GitHub organization of **Digital Media Factory (DMF)**.

### 🌐 About DMF
Digital Media Factory is a technology and media engineering company based in **Cairo** and **Dubai**. We specialize in building next-generation AI content engines, high-performance WebGL platforms, modern web applications, and enterprise mobile software. 

From synthetic video generation and dialect-aware voice synthesis to scalable cloud platforms, we bridge the gap between creative broadcast media and advanced software engineering.

- 🌐 **Website:** [digitalmediafactory.org](https://digitalmediafactory.org/)
- 📍 **Hubs:** Cairo, Egypt • Dubai, UAE
- 🎯 **Focus Areas:** AI Content Engines, Software Engineering, 3D WebGL, Mobile Development, and Broadcast Solutions.

---

### 🚀 Our Mission
To empower broadcasters, enterprises, and digital creators with intelligent software, purposeful design, and automated media pipelines. We believe in high performance, modular architecture, and technology built for measurable impact.

### ⚡ What We Do

#### 🧠 AI & Synthetic Media
*Smarter workflows and modern content generation:*
- High-fidelity synthetic video and automated presenter pipelines.
- Regional voice cloning & dubbing across 15+ Arabic dialects.
- Semantic video indexing and automated media analysis.

#### 🛠️ Software & Web Platforms
*Custom digital systems built for performance:*
- Immersive WebGL portals and 3D web experiences using Three.js and GSAP.
- Full-stack enterprise web platforms and back-office management dashboards.
- Scalable, high-throughput REST and GraphQL APIs.

#### 📱 Cross-Platform Mobile
*Seamless mobile experiences for iOS and Android:*
- High-performance mobile applications powered by Flutter.
- Real-time synchronization, offline-first data, and intuitive UI/UX.

#### 📦 Developer Tooling & SDKs
- Modular client SDKs, dynamic brand engines, and agent orchestration tooling.
